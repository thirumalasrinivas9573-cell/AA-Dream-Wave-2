## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an optimistic, restorative, and pristine environment engineered for high-performance sustainable energy and intelligent ecological monitoring. It merges biophilic clarity with advanced analytical confidence. The visual tone is brisk, radiant, and therapeutic—dispelling the clinical coldness of traditional dashboards in favor of an active, vibrant atmosphere that evokes renewable abundance and kinetic harmony.

The target audience spans clean-energy operators, enterprise ESG directors, and everyday users engaged with automated energy networks. The UI must evoke continuous momentum, relief, and validation through airy negative space, luminous organic gradients, and tactile soft-glass surfaces.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Bio-Minimalism** with **Luminous Soft-Glassmorphism**. Architectural purity governs the composition: pure white floating structural cards, crystalline mint diffusion layers, hyper-refined pill contours, and delicate green-tinted atmosphere rather than rigid structural divides.

## Colors

The palette establishes an invigorating, sunlit atmosphere anchored by deep organic forestry for high-contrast legibility.

- **Canvas & Backdrops:** The foundational surface is soft white-mint `#F6FBF8`, overlaid with atmospheric gradient sweeps shifting from `#CFE5D5` to `#F6FBF8` to simulate fresh daylight and natural filtration.
- **Surfaces & Cards:** Elevated surfaces are rendered in pure `#FFFFFF`, edged with a whisper-light translucent boundary of `rgba(110, 168, 158, 0.18)` and tinted ambient drop shadows.
- **Mint Scale (Atmospheric Tiers):**
  - Ultra Pale: `#E7F4EC` (subtle panel fills, hover highlights, chip backgrounds)
  - Soft Wash: `#CFE5D5` (canvas gradients, secondary highlights)
  - Sage Foam: `#B8D6B2` (inactive sliders, environmental accents)
  - Cool Mist: `#A6D2C8` (dividers, decorative soft lines)
  - Deep Mint: `#8FC6B7` (secondary icons, muted indicators)
- **Primary Teal:** `#2F8F83` (Interactive core, focused states, primary controls), shifting to `#267A70` on interactive hover.
- **Teal Mist Accent:** `#6EA89E` drives secondary interactive rings, gauge tracks, and radial progress arcs.
- **Typography & Content:**
  - Primary Text: Deep Forest Teal `#12332F` (near-black bio-contrast ensuring AAA compliance).
  - Secondary / Supporting Text: Mineral Sage `#4F6F69` (for meta-labels, timestamps, subtext).
- **Feedback & Validation:**
  - Active Alignment: `#2FBF71` (indicating nominal execution, energy surplus, and "ON TRACK" statuses).
  - Attention / Approval: `#F5A524` (reserved strictly for required authorizations, threshold alerts, and pending state transitions).

## Typography

The type system balances energetic visual structure with humanized, approachable clarity. 

- **Display & Headings (Lexend):** Lexend was engineered for fluid readability and optical decompression. Rendered in Semi-Bold (weight 600), it provides rhythmic cadence to major metric points, system declarations, and section titles without creating aggressive weight blocks.
- **Body & Functional UI (Be Vietnam Pro):** Modern, open, and mathematically balanced, Be Vietnam Pro maintains clean vertical proportions across dense telemetry grids, documentation panels, and interactive elements.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Large numerical data (such as kilowatt outputs or net-zero indices) must be rendered in Lexend Semi-Bold with tight tracking (`-0.03em`) accompanied by `label-md` or `label-sm` indicators set in uppercase with wide tracking (`0.06em`) for immediate peripheral scanability.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system embraces an open, fluid layout with deliberate macro-spacing to reinforce feelings of health, airiness, and clear operational overhead.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Desktop (1280px+): 12-column dynamic fluid grid, 2.5rem margins, 1.5rem gutters. Maximum container width capped at 1440px for comfortable reading spans.
  - Tablet (768px - 1279px): 8-column fluid grid, 2rem margins, 1.25rem gutters.
  - Mobile (up to 767px): 4-column fluid grid, 1.25rem margins, 1rem gutters.
- **Rhythm & Safe Insets:** Structural card padding enforces a minimum interior padding of `1.5rem` (`space-lg`) on compact elements and `2.5rem` (`space-xl`) on hero telemetry widgets, providing internal breathing room that prevents analytical elements from feeling crowded.
- **Reflow Rules:** Complex multi-card telemetry rows must stack or consolidate into horizontal gesture-based snap strips below 768px, ensuring that card corner radii and internal breathing room are never compressed.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through luminous, layered soft-glass optics and botanical ambient tinting, completely avoiding harsh achromatic drop shadows.

- **Base Layer (L0):** Canvas level with subtle linear gradients (`#CFE5D5` feathering into `#F6FBF8` at a 140° angle).
- **Floating Cards (L1):** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting on top of the gradient wash. Boundary defined by an ultra-thin 1px border of `rgba(110, 168, 158, 0.18)`. Shadow profile: 
  - `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(47, 143, 131, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(18, 51, 47, 0.03)`
- **Interactive Modals & Overlays (L2):** Glassmorphic surfaces with high-pass background diffusion (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), backed by `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` and illuminated by an inner border reflection:
  - `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -8px rgba(18, 51, 47, 0.12), inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`
- **Hover & Active Depth:** Elements elevate toward the viewer via an expanded mint diffusion aura (`0 16px 36px 0 rgba(47, 191, 113, 0.14)`), giving interactions a responsive, spring-like feedback loop.

## Shapes

The design system uses generous, organic, ultra-rounded forms to project an open, non-intimidating aesthetic.

- **Primary Geometry:** Default major containers and telemetry cards utilize full `24px` to `28px` corner radii (Token level 3 / Pill-shaped architecture), yielding pebble-smooth surfaces that feel sculpted and gentle.
- **Micro-Components:** Buttons, tags, chips, and text inputs employ complete pill borders (`border-radius: 9999px`) to match the curved aesthetic of the outer cards.
- **Nested Corner Preservation:** To maintain optical harmony when nesting elements within cards, the inner component radius must correspond to the parent radius minus the spacing offset ($R_{inner} = R_{outer} - Padding$), preventing awkward visual gaps and geometric friction.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in `#2F8F83` with `#FFFFFF` text. Fully pill-rounded (`border-radius: 9999px`), internal padding `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Hover triggers `#267A70` accompanied by a soft teal glow (`0 8px 20px rgba(47, 143, 131, 0.25)`). Active state scales subtly to `0.98`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Translucent mint fill `#E7F4EC` with `#2F8F83` typography. 1px matching border of `rgba(110, 168, 158, 0.25)`. On hover, the fill transitions to `#CFE5D5`.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped tags displaying dynamic system states.
- **"ON TRACK" Status Badge:** Tinted `#E7F4EC` background, `#2FBF71` crisp label text, housing a pulsing `6px` circular beacon in `#2FBF71` flanked by a 1px border of `rgba(47, 191, 113, 0.3)`.
- **Informational Metric Chips:** Height `28px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`, border `1px solid rgba(110, 168, 158, 0.2)`.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed from `#FFFFFF` using `24px` to `28px` radius, encased in a 1px `rgba(110, 168, 158, 0.18)` perimeter. 
- Integrated telemetry charts use `#6EA89E` and `#2F8F83` gradients for stroke fills, sitting directly on pure white surfaces to optimize contrast against the canvas wash.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Fully rounded (`border-radius: 9999px`), height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`. Bordered by `1.5px solid rgba(110, 168, 158, 0.3)`. 
- Placeholder text in `#4F6F69` at 60% opacity. 
- **Focus state:** Border dynamically transitions to `#2F8F83` with an outer mint halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px rgba(110, 168, 158, 0.20)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** Smooth rounded-rect (`8px`), border `2px solid #6EA89E`. Checked state transitions to `#2F8F83` solid fill with a crisp `#FFFFFF` icon mark.
- **Radios:** Outer circular ring in `#6EA89E`. Active state displays a `#2F8F83` inner core surrounded by `#FFFFFF` spacing.

### Approval & Alert Banners
- Used for high-priority operational confirmation.
- Subtle warm gradient background from `rgba(245, 165, 36, 0.08)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`, framed by a 1px amber border (`rgba(245, 165, 36, 0.4)`).
- Pill-shaped container holding an amber alert icon (`#F5A524`), `#12332F` headline text, and an immediate pill-shaped micro-action button.
